Runtime dependencies
Next.js and React. Nothing else.

The nameParticles in WebGL2, one draw call. A small liquid solver on a fixed clock, so 60 and 120 Hz screens see the same physics. It stops stepping when everything is still, and a window resize carries every particle's place and heat across.

The paperOne full-screen shader. Masses bend the grid through a softened potential and orbit by leapfrog integration, which does not slowly gain energy; their spiral-in is an exact decay, stable at any step. Ripples leave a pair at a finite speed, so every point of the grid sees the pair as it was when its ripple left.

EverythingLoops sleep off screen, in hidden tabs and when nothing moves. Nothing is stored on your device unless you say yes to the resident. TypeScript throughout, with unit tests for the physics and for every case-study miniature.
