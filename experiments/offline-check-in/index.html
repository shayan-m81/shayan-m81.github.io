EXP-04 · open source

Check-in that survives bad Wi-Fi

Ticket scanning that keeps working when the connection drops; online, the database refuses a second check-in.

Question
Can event staff keep scanning tickets when the connection drops, and still catch any ticket used twice?
My part
A side project.
Decision
Let the database refuse a second check-in, and make offline mode a short-lived signed grant rather than the default.It cost · Offline scanning has to be unlocked with a grant, and the grant runs out.
Result
  • Covered by Worker, D1, frontend, offline, concurrency and authorization tests.
a working miniature with every case, for the curious

The record

Context

A side project: party ticketing and QR check-in for event teams.

Setup

  • Cloudflare Workers and D1, with role-based access for admins and scanners.

Method

  • Database-enforced duplicate check-in protection.
  • An emergency offline mode for the primary scanner only, on IndexedDB and a Service Worker, unlocked by a short-lived signed grant.

Related

At work: I built, alone, the QR-code attendance feature of an employee transportation app that thousands of flexible warehouse staff use.

Instruments

Cloudflare Workers · D1 · TypeScript · IndexedDB · Service Workers
