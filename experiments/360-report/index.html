EXP-06 · work

Blind spots, drawn

The 360-degree feedback report: where a person's view of themselves and others' views of them differ, drawn.

Question
Can a 360-degree report show people, at a glance, where their own view and everyone else's differ?
My part
Built the report's front end, and reworked the evaluation flow that feeds it.
Decision
Show blind spots and strengths as views of their own, beside the radar, pie and metric views. My proposal; the designer approved it.It cost · Every view needs a definition of what counts as a gap; a threshold is a choice, not a fact.
Result
  • Built the report's front end and reworked the evaluation flow behind it.
a working miniature with every case, for the curious

The record

Context

360-degree feedback collects ratings about a person from several directions, including the person themselves. The report has to make the differences readable. It is part of the Talent and Performance products, which 2,000+ employees use.

Setup

  • The report's front end: radar, pie, metric, and blind-spot and strength views.

Method

  • Reworked the evaluation flow that feeds it: question types, nomination, team filters and report access control.

Side effects

Now sees radar charts in spider webs. Also the other way round.

Instruments

React · TypeScript · Data visualisation
